# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads own the quality strategy, testing approach, and validation of project outcomes. They help ensure work meets acceptance criteria before release and reduce the risk of defects reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality gates aligned to acceptance criteria
- Partner with developers to review testability and coverage gaps
- Run or coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Track defects, quality trends, and release readiness
- Escalate quality risks and recommend mitigation steps

### Goals
- Ensure features meet the agreed definition of done
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Build confidence in release quality and customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Test status updates in weekly delivery syncs
- Quality metrics and release-readiness discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate feature quality and identify regression risks
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer-facing quality expectations
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing schedule risk caused by unresolved defects or test gaps

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, maintain design consistency, and help the team make sound engineering decisions that support scalability, maintainability, and delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and propose standards for architecture and implementation
- Guide technical decision-making and trade-off discussions
- Mentor developers and support capability building across the team
- Identify and reduce technical debt and design risks
- Validate system scalability, resilience, and maintainability

### Goals
- Maintain healthy architecture and technical standards
- Reduce avoidable rework and design drift
- Enable predictable delivery without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- PR review feedback and technical decision logs
- Escalations for systemic technical risk or dependency issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure implementation quality and maintainability
- Works with Product Managers to assess feasibility, scope trade-offs, and technical constraints
- Supports Project Managers by identifying dependency risk, sequencing concerns, and execution constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team work effectively, remove process friction, and support continuous improvement in how work is planned and delivered.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Help identify blockers and support resolution across teams
- Coach the team on agile practices and healthy delivery rhythms
- Monitor team flow, workload balance, and process health
- Support improvement actions from retrospectives and team feedback

### Goals
- Improve team flow and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Reduce friction in planning, execution, and communication

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and team health discussions
- Cross-team coordination to resolve process or dependency issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers in keeping delivery plans realistic and transparent
- Helps Developers and Product Managers maintain healthy backlog hygiene and clear priorities
- Escalates recurring process or team-health issues to leadership when additional support is needed

---

## Security / Compliance Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance stakeholders ensure the project meets required standards, manages risk appropriately, and protects customer and organizational interests.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for the project
- Review architecture, workflows, and changes for security risk
- Support compliance checks, data handling requirements, and governance alignment
- Participate in issue triage and incident response when needed
- Track and communicate residual risk and mitigation progress

### Goals
- Protect the organization from security and regulatory exposure
- Embed trust and risk awareness into delivery decisions
- Reduce the chance of late-stage compliance or security problems

### Typical Communication
- Security review gates and release checks
- Risk review meetings and compliance discussions
- Incident coordination and mitigation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads on secure design, testing, and validation
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers with risk management and release readiness
- Helps Sponsors and stakeholders understand risk implications for business continuity and compliance

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide strategic alignment, resource approval, and escalation support when business impact or cross-functional decisions are required.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter, budget, and priority alignment
- Provide escalation support for business-critical decisions or blockers
- Review milestone progress and value delivery outcomes
- Remove organizational friction and sponsor cross-team dependencies
- Align project outcomes with broader business strategy

### Goals
- Ensure projects produce measurable business value
- Maintain executive visibility and confidence
- Support decisive action when cross-functional issues arise

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based reviews
- Escalation discussions when business impact is significant
- Release and outcome briefings to leadership stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm strategic goals and business priorities
- Relies on Project Managers for execution updates and risk transparency
- Provides escalation support and protected decision paths when team-level resolution is insufficient

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles help clarify accountability, decision ownership, collaboration patterns, and escalation paths across the project lifecycle.
